# MusicMatch

Team 8 - FLOWSTATE

MusicMatch is a personalized music discovery app where you swipe right to like a song and left to skip it, helping the app learn your taste in real time. With filters for genre, decade, artist, and more, MusicMatch delivers a curated stream of songs tailored to you. The more you interact, the smarter the recommendations become, removing songs you dislike and surfacing ones that match your vibe. You can easily build playlists from your likes and discover new music that feels uniquely yours.

We conducted initial interviews and found that the biggest issue with existing music apps is poor song recommendations. To better understand this, we carried out additional interviews focused on what users like, dislike, and want from a music recommendation experience. Three key needs emerged: filtering, personalization, and efficiency. Users want to filter music by attributes like genre, decade, and artist. They expect the app to adapt to their preferences by removing disliked songs and recommending similar ones to liked tracks. They also prefer quick previews to evaluate songs without listening from the beginning.

We then developed a low-fidelity prototype, which users generally liked in terms of design. However, testing revealed usability issues, particularly with the filtering page and playlist creation. Users also found the tutorial screens overwhelming. Based on this feedback, we refined the design, resulting in our current mid-fidelity prototype.
